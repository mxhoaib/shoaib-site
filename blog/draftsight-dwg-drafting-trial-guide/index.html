DraftSight DWG drafting is a common search for freelancers who want a familiar 2D CAD seat without jumping straight to a full AutoCAD subscription. DraftSight can open and edit DWG files, but UAE shop-drawing work lives or dies on Xrefs, fonts, CTB plot styles and what the consultant’s review machine expects.

This legal guide covers official trial setup, practical 2D limits, and a clear decision matrix against AutoCAD LT and BricsCAD so you spend once and avoid file-rejection loops.

Key takeaways

  • Always trial DraftSight on a real multi-Xref package before buying.
  • Stay on AutoCAD LT when consultants mandate it.
  • Compare total cost: seat + rejected-submission risk.
  • LibreCAD is fine for light DXF—not heavy UAE shop sets.

Official downloads and alternatives

Start at DraftSight.com for current editions and trials. Compare against AutoCAD LT and BricsCAD (Bricsys). For a free DXF-oriented editor, see LibreCAD—useful for light 2D, not a full shop-drawing seat.

Trial setup checklist

  1. Install only from draftsight.com.
  2. Open a previous approved package with nested Xrefs.
  3. Confirm layer colours and our UAE AutoCAD layer standards still plot correctly.
  4. Load your CTB and plot one sheet—see CTB plot style guidance.
  5. Round-trip the DWG into AutoCAD LT (or the consultant’s version) and re-plot.
  6. Only then commit the seat for client work.

When to stay on AutoCAD LT

  • The CAD manual names AutoCAD explicitly.
  • You deliver Dynamic/Smart Blocks the client edits in AutoCAD.
  • Main contractor CDE rejects non-Autodesk saved DWGs historically.
  • Your team already shares Connected Support Files on Autodesk seats—see AutoCAD 2026 new features.

Freelance delivery checklist

Before you tell a Fiverr or agency client “I use DraftSight,” run this:

  • Ask which AutoCAD year they review on.
  • Send a sample DWG + PDF from your DraftSight seat for their approval.
  • Confirm font package (SHX/TTF) travels with the zip.
  • Agree whether Dynamic Blocks must remain editable on their seat.
  • Price one revision round caused by file-compatibility issues into the quote.

If any answer is uncertain, default to AutoCAD LT for that client and keep DraftSight for internal sketching only.

Office policy template (copy/adapt)

  1. Production shop drawings: AutoCAD LT or full AutoCAD unless Project Manager approves DraftSight in writing.
  2. Every DraftSight project must archive a round-trip test PDF from the client’s CAD version.
  3. No mixed plot styles across seats on the same package.
  4. Templates and fonts live in one read-only folder; local copies are forbidden.
  5. Annual review of seat mix vs rejected-file incidents.

Put that policy in the same place as your layer DWT. Freelancers joining mid-project need the rule on day one, not after a failed submission.

Cost risks beyond the licence price

  • One rejected authority or consultant package can cost more than a year of LT.
  • Training time for a unique UI quirk shows up as overtime on deadlines.
  • Plugin ecosystems differ; a Lisp that saved hours on AutoCAD may need rewriting.
  • Hiring temporary draftsmen is easier when the seat is AutoCAD-shaped.

Run the numbers with those soft costs included. DraftSight can still win—just win for the right reasons after a successful trial, not because the sticker price looked lower on day one.

Summary recommendation

Use DraftSight when your verified round-trips succeed and clients accept the seat. Use AutoCAD LT when UAE consultants, main contractors or CAD manuals effectively require it. Use BricsCAD when you need DWG plus optional 3D growth and have tested the same way. Mention LibreCAD only for light DXF tasks. That hierarchy keeps freelancers billable and packages approvable.

FAQ

Is DraftSight good for DWG shop drawings?

DraftSight can draft and edit DWG for many 2D tasks. For UAE shop drawings, confirm the consultant accepts your saved DWG version and that Xrefs, CTB and fonts behave identically to their AutoCAD-based review.

DraftSight vs AutoCAD LT — which should freelancers buy?

If clients and main contractors expect AutoCAD LT workflows, stay on LT. Consider DraftSight when budget is tight and you have verified file round-trips on real project samples.

Is LibreCAD a full AutoCAD replacement?

LibreCAD is a free DXF-oriented option useful for light 2D work. It is not a drop-in replacement for complex DWG shop-drawing packages with heavy Xrefs and plot styles.
