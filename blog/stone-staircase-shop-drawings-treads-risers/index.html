Stone staircase shop drawings fail reviews when treads, risers and fixings are pretty but incomplete. Consultants in the UAE expect clear dimensions, nosing profiles, thicknesses, support conditions and installation sequence—especially on marble or limestone stairs in villas and lobbies.

This how-to covers sheet content, detailing callouts, anchor coordination and annotative scaling so your stair package clears shop-drawing approval without endless “provide fixing detail” comments.

Key takeaways

  • Dimension every tread/riser; do not rely on a single typical alone when runs differ.
  • Show fixings with substrate and engineer sign-off references.
  • Tag pieces for fabrication order and nest them—see slab nesting post.
  • Check Dubai Building Code accessibility geometry.

Sheet set contents

  1. General notes: stone type, finish, thickness, sealers, tolerances.
  2. Plan of stair with tread numbers matching fabrication tags.
  3. Side section through going and landing.
  4. Nosing and edge profiles at large scale.
  5. Fixing details at wall stringer / steel stringer / concrete.
  6. Landing slab joints and movement joints.

Fabrication handoff

After approval, nest treads and risers with vein control using marble slab nesting and CNC DXF.

Field measure protocol before cutting stone

  1. Laser the finished floor levels at top and bottom landings after screed.
  2. Check structural stringer or concrete waist tolerances vs GA.
  3. Confirm handrail centreline so nosings are not cut short.
  4. Update the shop drawing revision; do not “adjust on site” silently.
  5. Only then release the CNC DXF nest.

Skipping field measure is the fastest way to shatter a book-matched feature stair.

Tolerance and joint notes (starter text)

Adapt this note block to the project specification and Natural Stone Institute guidance:

  • Joint width typically 2–3 mm unless the consultant specifies otherwise.
  • Lippage limits per finish type; polished floors need tighter control.
  • Movement joints at landings as directed by the structural engineer.
  • Protect edges during install; chipped nosings are not “site repair with filler” on premium marble.

Coordination with structure and MEP

Stone stairs share space with lighting, sprinklers and steel. Before issuing SDs:

  • Confirm soffit lights do not clash with tread thickness.
  • Check sprinkler heads under landings against soft clearance.
  • Verify steel stringer camber assumptions with the fabricator.
  • Run a quick clash mindset even if the stair is “only 2D”—see our Navisworks workflow when a full model exists.

Revision discipline

Each field-measure update gets a new revision cloud on changed treads only. Update the nest revision in lockstep. Email the revision letter to consultant and fabricator the same day; do not rely on chat apps for controlled documents.

Sample annotation set (metric)

  • Tread: 300 × 1200 × 30 mm, bullnose R10, polished Carrara (sample ID).
  • Riser: 150 × 1200 × 20 mm, honed, same batch.
  • Landing slab: joints aligned to grid; movement joint per engineer detail ST-xx.
  • Wall fixing: stainless anchor type per cladding engineer drawing CL-xx; spacing 400 mm max c/c (confirm).
  • Install sequence: wall stringer plates → landings → treads bottom-up → nosing protection.

Replace sample IDs with project values. The structure of the note matters as much as the numbers.

Handover to install crew

Issue a pocket PDF: tread tags, start point, protection notes, adhesive/anchor references, and a photo key of finished samples. Crews that only receive a full A1 set on a dusty site make more mistakes than crews with a simplified install sheet plus the controlled SD on the CDE.

Lighting and shadow lines

Show nosing shadow gaps when the design uses floating treads. Missing that reveal dimension is a frequent site argument. Coordinate LED strip recesses with the electrical shop drawings so stone is not cut twice.

FAQ

What should stone staircase shop drawings include?

Plans and sections with tread/riser dimensions, nosing profile, thickness, material, fixings/anchors, stringer or wall support conditions, sequence tags and any structural engineer notes required by the consultant.

How do fixings appear on UAE stair SDs?

Show anchor type, embedment, spacing and substrate (concrete, steel stringer, wall). Coordinate with the structural or façade engineer—do not invent capacities on the drafting sheet.

Are accessibility rules relevant to stone stairs?

Yes. Riser/tread geometry and handrail clearances must respect the applicable Dubai Building Code and project accessibility requirements. Confirm values on official DM code pages.
