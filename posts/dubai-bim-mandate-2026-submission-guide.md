The **Dubai BIM mandate 2026** is the compliance topic every consultant, contractor and drafting studio should brief before mid-year bids. Dubai Municipality communications around Circular materials describe mandatory BIM for qualifying buildings from 1 April 2026—commonly summarised as projects with 12 or more floors or 15,000 m² and above, with additional modification triggers. Always read the official circular text; thresholds and definitions can be refined in authority guidance.

This article is a practical prep guide: who is likely in scope, what to assemble in the BIM Execution Plan (BEP), and how shop drawings, IFC and Build in Dubai submissions connect—without replacing legal advice or the DM portal itself.

## Prep checklist for consultants and drafting studios

1. **BEP draft:** software versions, shared coordinates, naming, clash tolerances, issue tracker.
2. **Authoring choice:** Revit or Archicad workflows—see [Revit BIM for small design firms](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2026/10/revit-bim-workflow-for-small-design.html) and [Archicad vs Revit](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2026/10/archicad-vs-revit-which-bim-tool-fits.html).
3. **IFC path:** practise export and validation—full guide: [IFC OpenBIM for Dubai BIM submissions](../../blog/ifc-openbim-workflow-dubai-bim-submissions/).
4. **Clash process:** Navisworks or equivalent—see [Navisworks clash detection UAE](../../blog/navisworks-clash-detection-uae-coordination/).
5. **Shop drawings:** keep 2D packages aligned to the model; follow [shop drawing approval in Dubai](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2026/10/shop-drawing-approval-process-dubai.html) and [fit-out approval drawings](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2026/10/dubai-fit-out-approval-drawings.html).
6. **Site/civil:** if Civil 3D feeds the site model, align early—[Civil 3D basics](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2026/10/civil-3d-basics-for-site-and.html).
7. **Design quality:** BIM does not replace design judgment—[art and science of architectural design](https://shoaibsite.blogspot.com/2023/12/the-art-and-science-of-architectural.html).

## 90-day studio action plan

- Week 1–2: read circular + appoint BIM lead.
- Week 3–6: BEP template + IFC pilot on a past project.
- Week 7–10: clash matrix + CDE folder structure.
- Week 11–12: fee proposals updated with BIM line items.

## Fee proposal language (starter)

Studios often underprice BIM. Consider explicit lines such as:

- BIM Execution Plan preparation and updates.
- IFC export and validation cycles (state number of drops).
- Clash detection support hours (or coordination attendance).
- Model-to-shop-drawing consistency checks.

State exclusions clearly: authority portal fees, third-party model checking licences, and redesign caused by client brief changes after option freeze.

## What “modification triggers” mean in practice

Existing buildings sometimes fall into BIM scope when a major modification is proposed. Practical questions for the design lead:

- Does the modification exceed area or complexity thresholds in the circular?
- Is a new tower wing treated as a separate building?
- Do fit-out-only works inside an already-permitted tower require full BIM, or only when DM says so?

Answer those from the official text and, if needed, a formal authority query—not from informal chat rumours (and never publish phone-based advice on this blog).

Document the interpretation in the project BEP so every subconsultant follows the same assumption.

## Communication plan inside the studio

Brief every discipline lead before April 2026: what changes in fees, software, and delivery dates. Share a one-page FAQ internally so sales does not promise 2D-only packages on projects that clearly meet BIM thresholds. Update proposal templates the same week the internal brief goes out.

## FAQ

### Who must submit BIM under Dubai’s 2026 rules?

Projects meeting Dubai Municipality’s published thresholds—commonly referenced as buildings with 12 or more floors or 15,000 m² and above, plus applicable modification triggers—should plan for mandatory BIM. Always confirm the current circular text on official DM channels.

### When does the Dubai BIM mandate start?

Public communications point to 1 April 2026 for the mandatory BIM regime described in DM circular materials. Verify the exact commencement and scope on Dubai Municipality’s website before bidding or contracting.

### What should consultants prepare now?

Agree a BEP, choose authoring tools, practise IFC export/checks, align naming and coordinates, and connect the model workflow to Build in Dubai submissions and shop-drawing approvals.
