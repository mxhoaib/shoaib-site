Questions.

Straight answers before you order.

How do I hire you?

The quickest way is through my Fiverr profile at fiverr.com/mxhoaib, where you can message me, request a custom offer or order a package. You can also email mxhoaib0@gmail.com.

Which countries do you work with?

I work remotely with clients in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and the United Arab Emirates, as well as anywhere else drawings can be shared online.

What files do I need to send?

Whatever you have: site measurements, sketches, photos, PDFs or DWGs, plus material and finish details. I will tell you if anything important is missing.

What file formats do you deliver?

Editable AutoCAD DWG, DXF for CNC, plotted PDF sets, and JPG/PNG for renders. Other formats on request.

Do you work in metric or imperial units?

Both. Millimetres are standard in the UAE, UK and Canada; feet and inches are common in the USA. Tell me which your project uses.

How long does a shop drawing take?

It depends on size and how complete the inputs are. Simple vanity or countertop drawings can be quick; full stone packages take longer. Send your files for an estimate.

How are revisions handled?

Revisions are included as described in each Fiverr package. Changes are clouded and recorded in the revision table so reviewers can see what changed.

Is my project information kept confidential?

Yes. Your drawings are used only for your project, and I can delete files after delivery on request.

Do you prepare drawings for Dubai consultant approval?

Yes. I have eight years of drafting experience in the UAE and prepare submissions in the formats Dubai consultants and main contractors expect.

Do you offer quantity take-off and BOQ services?

Yes. I prepare quantity take-offs, bills of quantities and cost estimates from PDF or DWG drawings, including detailed marble and stone quantities.

Can you tell me how many marble slabs to order?

Yes. I measure the net stone area, edges and cut-outs and lay pieces out against your slab sizes to estimate the slab count, so you can order with confidence.

Do you only work through Fiverr?

Fiverr is the main way to hire me because it protects both sides with secure payments. For questions before ordering, email mxhoaib0@gmail.com.
