Common questions

What do marble shop drawings include?

Layout plans, elevations, sections, edge and fixing details, piece drawings and a cutting list, all referenced with consistent piece IDs.

Can you work from site measurements only?

Yes. A hand sketch with measurements and photos is enough to start; I will flag anything missing before drawing.
