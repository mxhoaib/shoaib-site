# Venatino Marble Freestanding Bath Vanity Shop Drawing – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

A fabrication shop drawing for a freestanding bathroom vanity in 2 cm Venatino marble, made for a private villa in Dubai. The vanity is 2200 mm long and stands on brass legs. It has an undermounted oval basin, a bottle shelf, a moulded fascia and a lower display shelf.

The sheet includes a front elevation, a side section, the vanity top plan and the shelf top plan. Every dimension is given for cutting and assembly. Four enlarged details (D1 to D4) show the moulding profiles, the pencil-polish and full-round edges, the undermount sink junction, the backsplash and the support brackets.

The marble pieces (C-01 to C-06 and S-01 to S-04) are placed directly on photos of the approved slabs. The client and fabricator can then confirm vein direction and matching before any cut is made.

Deliverables: one fully dimensioned shop drawing sheet with elevations, sections, edge details and a slab-mapped cutting plan.

**Software:** AutoCAD 2021
